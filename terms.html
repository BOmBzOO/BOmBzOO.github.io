2. Description of Service

BombZoo Studio is an open web-based video preparation and publishing platform designed for independent educators, science communicators, researchers, and financial literacy creators. The Service allows any registered or authenticated creator to connect their own TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, preview short-form educational videos without watermarks, edit post titles and academic citations, configure privacy and interaction settings, disclose commercial or AI-generated content, view their recently published TikTok posts, and publish videos directly to their TikTok profile or send drafts to their TikTok inbox.

3. User Control & Explicit Publishing Consent

  • Creators retain 100% ownership and control over the videos and captions they prepare and upload through BombZoo Studio.
  • BombZoo Studio never posts content automatically without the Creator's explicit action. Every Direct Post or Inbox Draft upload requires the Creator to manually select privacy settings, configure interaction and commercial disclosure toggles, review the live video preview, and click the publish button.
  • BombZoo Studio does not superimpose any third-party brand logos, watermarks, or promotional overlays onto Creator videos.

4. Prohibited Conduct

  • Uploading content that infringes copyright, trademark, or intellectual property rights of third parties.
  • Posting unverified medical misinformation, spam, or content that violates TikTok Community Guidelines.
  • Attempting to bypass TikTok's commercial content disclosure requirements or rate limits.

5. Account Disconnection & Termination

Creators may disconnect their TikTok account at any time inside the BombZoo Studio interface or revoke permissions directly within the TikTok mobile app under Settings and privacy › Security and permissions › Apps and services permissions.
